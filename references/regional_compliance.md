# Regional Telephony Compliance & Boundaries

## E.164 Formatting
All phone numbers passed to `CALL-E` must conform to `+[country code][subscriber number]`.

## Consent & Recording Regulations
- **Two-Party Consent States/Countries:** In jurisdictions requiring explicit recording consent, the caller prompt includes a mandatory disclosure statement.
- **Operating Hours:** Outbound calls must strictly respect local time windows (e.g., 09:00 AM to 08:00 PM recipient local time).
